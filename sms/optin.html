Opt In

Sign up for SMS timecard notifications

This form is for current Capital Excavation employees and authorized team members in field leadership roles: foremen, superintendents, division managers, area managers, and payroll administration. Your request is routed to Capital Excavation IT for enrollment. You can also opt in verbally with your supervisor or Capital Excavation IT.

By enrolling you agree to receive recurring SMS timecard notifications from Capital Excavation. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel at any time. Consent is not a condition of employment. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
