SMS Campaign Registration

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

This page contains the campaign registration details for Capital Excavation's SMS timecard notification program, prepared for submission in the Teams admin center (Voice > Service Configuration > SMS > Step 2: Create Campaign). Each section below maps to a field in the campaign form.

Campaign Use Case

Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS), Low Volume. This use case is defined by Microsoft and cannot be changed. The registration covers SMS-enabled phone numbers assigned to individual employees for 1-to-1 conversations only; it does not support marketing, bulk messaging, automated alerts, or API-driven interactions.

Campaign Description

Paste into: Campaign Description
Capital Excavation sends SMS timecard notifications to employees in field leadership roles: foremen, superintendents, division managers, area managers, and payroll administration. The program sends scheduled, conditional operational notifications connected to our timecard system, including timecard submission reminders, approval deadline notices, escalation alerts for missing or unapproved timecards, anomaly-flag reports requiring resolution, and payroll export status updates. Messages are 1-to-1 and low volume, and are sent only to employees who have an outstanding timecard action item. Participation is entirely optional. Recipients opt in by providing their mobile number and giving separate, affirmative consent to receive timecard notification texts. Declining SMS does not affect employment, account access, timecard access, services, purchases, or transactions; reminders remain available in the timecard system and through supervisors. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for support at any time. This program does not support marketing, bulk messaging, or API-driven consumer interactions.

Call to Action

Opt-in is collected verbally or through the web opt-in form at https://apps.capitalexcavation.com/sms/optin.html. The web form uses an unchecked, standalone SMS consent checkbox and provides a visible "No thanks — continue without SMS" option. SMS consent is not bundled with legal or service terms.

Paste into: Call to Action
Capital Excavation collects opt-in verbally at its locations and through the web form at https://apps.capitalexcavation.com/sms/optin.html. The web form presents a standalone, unchecked checkbox used only for affirmative SMS consent. The checkbox is not bundled with acceptance of Terms and Conditions, the Privacy Policy, employment terms, account creation, purchases, transactions, or access to services. A visible "No thanks - continue without SMS" option lets employees decline without submitting information. Declining SMS does not affect employment, account access, timecard access, services, purchases, or transactions; timecard reminders remain available in the timecard system and through supervisors. Employees who opt in provide their mobile number and consent to receive recurring timecard notification text messages from Capital Excavation, including timecard submission reminders, approval deadline notices, escalation alerts, anomaly-flag reports, and payroll export status updates. Employees are informed that "Message and data rates may apply", "Message frequency varies", and they can "text HELP for support or STOP to unsubscribe". Phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Submission Notes

  • A campaign can be submitted while its status is Draft or Pending Customer Update, with a maximum of four submissions; after the fourth attempt the campaign is permanently denied.
  • Once approved, campaign content and configuration cannot be changed.
  • Review by 10DLC operators may take up to 20 business days.
  • If more than 49 SMS-enabled numbers are needed, request a number pool while the campaign is still in Draft or Pending Customer Update.
