SMS Campaign Registration

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

This page contains the Twilio A2P campaign registration wording for Capital Excavation’s SMS timecard notification program.

Messaging Program

Voluntary operational SMS timecard notifications delivered through Twilio. No marketing. No bulk messaging.

Campaign Description

Paste into: Campaign Description
Capital Excavation sends SMS timecard notifications to employees in field leadership roles — foremen, superintendents, division and area managers, and payroll administration — through Twilio. Messages are 1-to-1 operational notifications tied to our timecard system. No marketing. No bulk messaging.

How do end-users consent to receive messages?

End users are Capital Excavation employees in field leadership roles. There is exactly one method of collecting consent for this campaign: the web opt-in form at https://sms.capitalexcavation.com/sms/optin.html. No chat widget, paper form, verbal process, offline process, or other page collects consent. There are no opt-in keywords; consent cannot be initiated by text message. START is supported only as the CTIA-required means for an employee who previously replied STOP to resume the messages they had already consented to on the web form; it restores a prior consent and never creates a new enrollment. A number that has not completed the web form is never enrolled by texting START. On that page the employee enters their own mobile number and their role, then actively checks a single, standalone, unchecked SMS consent checkbox labeled: "I choose to receive recurring SMS timecard notifications from Capital Excavation at the mobile number provided, including submission reminders, approval notices, escalation alerts, anomaly reports, and payroll export updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance." The consent checkbox is not pre-checked and is not bundled with any other agreement. It is not combined with acceptance of the Terms and Conditions, the Privacy Policy, employment terms, account creation, a purchase, or a transaction. Directly beside the submit button the page shows a visible decline option, "No thanks - skip SMS enrollment", which leaves the form and sends nothing. The page states that SMS enrollment is optional, that consent is not a condition of employment, and that declining or stopping messages does not affect employment, access to the timecard system, timecard submission or approval, or payroll processing. Submitting the form opens a pre-filled email in the employee's own mail client addressed to admin@capitalexcavation.com containing the employee's name, mobile number, role, the full consent statement above, and a timestamp. The website itself is static: it stores and transmits nothing. Consent is created by the employee sending that email. Capital Excavation IT retains that email as the consent record, including the date, mobile number, and consent language, and enrolls the number only after receiving it. Above the form, employees are informed that messages include timecard submission reminders, approval deadline notices, escalation alerts, anomaly-flag reports, and payroll export status updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. No marketing messages are sent. No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Enrollment Records

This site is static and stores no consent records. The opt-in form prepares an email in the employee's own mail client; consent is created when the employee sends it. Capital Excavation IT retains that email as the consent record — it carries the mobile number, the employee's name and role, the full consent language, and a timestamp — and enrolls the number only after receiving it. Retain the email with the enrollment record for the life of the enrollment and for a reasonable period afterward.

The web opt-in form is the only enrollment path for this campaign. Do not enroll a number on a verbal request, an unsolicited text, a phone number supplied without consent language, or any other channel.
