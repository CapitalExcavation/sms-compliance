SMS Campaign Registration

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This page contains the Twilio A2P campaign registration wording for Capital Excavation’s SMS timecard notification program.

Messaging Program

Voluntary operational SMS timecard notifications delivered through Twilio. No marketing. No bulk messaging.

Campaign Description

Paste into: Campaign Description
Capital Excavation sends SMS timecard notifications to employees in field leadership roles — foremen, superintendents, division and area managers, and payroll administration — through Twilio. Messages are 1-to-1 operational notifications tied to our timecard system. No marketing. No bulk messaging.

How do end-users consent to receive messages?

Capital Excavation’s SMS timecard notification program is voluntary. SMS consent is not a condition of employment, access to the timecard system, timecard submission or approval, or payroll processing. Employees who decline or withdraw SMS consent can continue using the timecard system. Employees opt in at https://capitalexcavation.github.io/sms-compliance/sms/optin.html by entering their mobile number and actively selecting an unchecked SMS consent checkbox. Employees may also opt in verbally with their supervisor or Capital Excavation IT, or by emailing admin@capitalexcavation.com. For verbal enrollment, staff explain the program and disclosures below and ask for an explicit affirmative response before enrolling the employee. For email enrollment, the employee must explicitly request SMS timecard notifications; providing a phone number alone does not constitute consent. Capital Excavation records the date, method, mobile number, and consent. Before enrollment, employees are informed that messages include timecard submission reminders, approval deadline notices, escalation alerts, anomaly-flag reports, and payroll export status updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. No marketing messages are sent. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Sample Messages

Sample messages reflect the notifications the program sends. Every sample includes the registered brand name (Capital Excavation) and opt-out instructions. No sample includes an embedded link or embedded phone number, consistent with the content attributes below.
