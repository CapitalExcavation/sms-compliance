SMS Timecard Notification Program

Operational text notifications for Capital Excavation field leadership

Capital Excavation sends SMS timecard notifications to employees in field leadership roles — foremen, superintendents, division and area managers, and payroll administration — through Microsoft Teams. Messages are 1-to-1 operational notifications tied to our timecard system. No marketing. No bulk messaging.

What you'll receive

Submission reminders

Timecard submission reminders and approval deadline notices, sent only when you have an outstanding action item.

Escalation alerts

Alerts for missing or unapproved timecards that need attention before payroll deadlines.

Anomaly flags

Reports of flagged timecard anomalies that require review or resolution in the timecard system.

Payroll export status

Status updates when payroll exports run, including counts of unapproved cards for the pay week.

Text keywords

Reply to any program message with these keywords at any time:

STOP

Unsubscribe immediately. You'll get a one-time confirmation and no further messages.

HELP

Get support information, including our support email address.

START

Re-subscribe after opting out and resume timecard notifications.

How opt-in works

  1. You provide your mobile number to your supervisor or Capital Excavation IT — verbally or through the opt-in form — and consent to receive timecard notifications.
  2. You receive a one-time confirmation message with program details and opt-out instructions.
  3. You receive timecard notifications only when you have an outstanding timecard action item. Message frequency varies.
  4. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
