SMS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Capital Excavation ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information related to our internal SMS messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to our SMS messaging program, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your opt-in consent and date
  • Messages sent to and from the program

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile number solely to send internal team communications, including but not limited to:

  • Team coordination and scheduling messages
  • Operational updates and alerts
  • Internal announcements

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile opt-in data or consent with any third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information may be shared only with our SMS service provider for the purpose of delivering messages, and as required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your mobile number and opt-in records for as long as you are enrolled in the messaging program and for a reasonable period thereafter as required for legal or compliance purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message
  • Request information about the data we hold by contacting us
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
